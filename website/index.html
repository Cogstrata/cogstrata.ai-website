How it works

1

Collect & link

Data You Can Reason With

Every neighbourhood maps to one of 24 groups, identified by a three-part label drawn from a fixed vocabulary. No codebook required — the label is the description.

Alongside the label, every record includes the supergroup, a plain-English descriptor, structured trait tags for filtering and segmentation, and a pre-written AI summary ready for direct injection into an agent's context window.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Cogstrata

How is Cogstrata different from other data providers?

Most providers sell static annual snapshots. Cogstrata recalibrates continuously against live macro signals — inflation, rates, employment, energy prices — so profiles reflect current reality. Our attribute set also includes markers legacy products don't carry, such as deprivation scores, food-desert flags, banking-access and crime-risk indicators, derived from curated open data.

What data attributes are included?

Six categories: Location & Classification, Wealth & Deprivation, Housing & Property, Education & Skills, Household Composition, and Mobility & Retail Access. Full data dictionary available on request.

How do I access the data?

Four options: real-time REST API, batch file export, cloud marketplace listings (AWS Data Exchange, Snowflake), or direct CRM/CDP integration. Our team recommends the best fit for your stack.

Is the data GDPR compliant?

Yes. All data is aggregated at area level — no individual PII. We hold no personal data. Fully compliant with GDPR and equivalent regional privacy frameworks.

How do I get started?

Register your interest and we'll set up a free sample run against your own data within 24 hours. No contract needed to evaluate. You'll see results immediately.
